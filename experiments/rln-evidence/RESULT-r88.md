## External control for single-arm RLN trials (consequence)
Switch-effect CR prediction interval for a new cohort: 0.10-0.74 (tau2 0.58).
A single-arm trial must show CR >= 95% (n=20), 91% (n=35), 87% (n=53) to clearly beat switching.
Overall response cannot discriminate at any n tested.
A patient-level external-control model explaining 50/75/90% of the spread lowers the n=35 bar to 83/80/74%.
The residual floor comes from having only 6 switch cohorts: more switch data, patient-level, is the lever.
