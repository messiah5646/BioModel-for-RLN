# Round 89 result: blood before a flare (GSE254176)
All four pre-registered tests FAIL (6 episodes / 3 run-up pairs):
- IFN Flare>Before 2/6; Before>Remission 2/3. Plasmablast Flare>Before 4/6; Before>Remission 2/3.
Diagnostics: no C6/C8 batch effect. Patient identity explains 54% of IFN variance; state means 1.14/1.09/1.11.
Literature agrees (Petri 2009 Lupus; Landolt-Marticorena 2009 ARD): IFN score stable within patients, does not track
flares. It does fall after deep B-cell depletion (rounds ifn-cart: CAR-T 6/7, CD3xCD19 8/11 at M3) and in treatment
responders (KURDS, ARD 2026).
Model consequence: IFN = slow patient-specific set point, moved by removing the B-cell/immune-complex driver;
not a flare-state variable and not a flare early-warning marker in blood.
