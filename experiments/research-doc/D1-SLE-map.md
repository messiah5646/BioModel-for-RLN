Main finding: SLE is a years-long preclinical process (autoantibodies in 88% up to 9.4 y before diagnosis,
NEJM 2003; IFN-gamma dysregulation precedes autoantibody accrual, type I IFN-alpha and BLyS rise only shortly
before classification, ARD 2016) that becomes a self-sustaining loop: clearance failure -> nucleic-acid sensing
(TLR7/9, cGAS-STING) -> type I IFN -> CXCL13+ Tfh/Tph and extrafollicular (DN2/ABC) B cells -> immune complexes
-> pDC IFN and NETosis (loop closes) -> organ injury. Monogenic causes each break one step (C1q/C4, DNASE1L3,
TREX1, TLR7 Y264H, UNC93B1). X dosage via TLR7 escape from X inactivation and XIST RNP autoantigens; EBNA2 binds
~half of SLE risk loci. 82.6% of TULIP patients IFN-high; plasmablasts, not IFN, track activity (Banchereau 2016).
Damage (SDI) is absorbing, partly steroid-driven, predicts death.

damage = absorbing state with activity and toxicity inputs (k_dam, k_tox). Predictions route to Q30, Q31, Q32, new Q55.
