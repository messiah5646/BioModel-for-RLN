# Q4 / Round 93: D7 responsive vs refractory LN (literature synthesis)

Written into the claude.ai research doc "SLE, LN and refractory LN: mechanistic reconstruction"
(artifact Mv7f8uVm8ELsioUZqncEBH), tab "D7: Responsive vs refractory", 2026-11-21.

No pre-registered test: this item is a literature synthesis, not a model test. Nothing was scored.

Main finding: responders and non-responders barely differ at baseline (clinical baseline and
3 kidney PCs both at chance in round 91); they diverge by month 3-6. Non-response = four separable
failures: under-exposure (MPA AUC, anti-rituximab ADA), incomplete engagement (LUNAR: CR 47% with
complete B-cell depletion vs 13% without), persistent interstitial myeloid activity (Parikh 2022;
round 91), damage floor (chronicity >=4, IF/TA, APOL1).

Proposed testable predictions (not tested): exposure gate (+0.05 AUC over UPCR at month 3);
interstitial programme replication (Q12); damage floor as a fixed proteinuria term; urine beats blood (Q9).
